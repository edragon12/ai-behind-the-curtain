Related tools

The same upload can be met at three different points. ECP is a generic control on the laptop and stops it at the tool call. Infoblox (DNS security) can block the DNS lookup of the unknown domain before the connection is made. Kentik (network observability) detects and alerts on the unusual outbound flow. It does not block the upload on the laptop. The network tools do not replace ECP.
